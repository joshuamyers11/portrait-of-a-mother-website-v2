The heart of the memoir

Sometimes understanding
arrives late.

She became a mother of five by twenty-one. To many, she could appear difficult. To her children, the hurt was real.

But a person is never only the pain they cause. In this deeply personal memoir, Edith L. Gadson revisits a childhood shaped by poverty, a complicated marriage, fierce love, and wounds her mother never learned to name.

Moving through memory rather than chronology, Portrait of a Mother asks what happens when we finally see the story beneath someone’s behavior—and how faith can transform what suffering means.

Meet the debut author

Edith L.
Gadson

Edith writes from the difficult, tender place where memory, family, and faith meet.

In her debut memoir, she offers an honest and respectful portrait of her mother’s life—holding love beside hurt, and truth beside grace. Her hope is to reach anyone who has carried pain, regardless of where they are on their own journey of faith.
